Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Unlock & Color (the “Game”), provided by Techno Creative Company (“we”, “us”), does with your information. Personal data is handled in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Playing as a guest

You can play without giving us any personal information.

If you create an account

The Game stores your name, your email address (or the user ID that Google provides), an optional profile picture (accounts only, not guests), and your progress, coins and statistics. In this version of the Game this is saved only on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to our servers.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, you are taken to Google, which handles your login under its own privacy policy and tells the Game your name, email address and a user ID. We never see your Google password. For email accounts, your password is stored on your device only in scrambled (hashed) form.

Data saved in your browser

Your browser may save your language choice, your coin score, any translations you requested, and your acceptance of the Terms on your own device (local storage). This is only used to make the Game work and remember your choices. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Translations

Some languages are translated automatically on request. Only the Game’s own text is sent for translation. The Game has no text boxes, so nothing you type is sent. The translation service may process this text outside Switzerland.

Hosting

The Game is hosted by a third-party web host (GitHub Pages), which may collect technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. This may involve processing outside Switzerland.

Your choices

You can log out, or delete your account and all its saved data, at any time from the Stats screen. Clearing your browser data also removes it.

Children

The Game is for all ages. Players under 18 should use it with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Contact

Questions about your data? Contact Techno Creative Company, Switzerland, at technocreativecompany@gmail.com.
